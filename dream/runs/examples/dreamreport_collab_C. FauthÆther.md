# Dream title
Lowering Field with Sleeper and Red Opening

# Dream
The black floor keeps changing what it is while I remember it: hot lacquer with red held under the surface, then dark water holding still long enough to bear weight, then gloss again wherever the sleeper exhales. The room begins in a dim metallic afterglow with salt, ozone, wet stone, and fine dust in the air, and everything already seems to have accepted a downward direction before it has accepted a stable form.

The red opening remains one of the clearest things, though it never settles into only one place. It stands ahead as a vertical wound or parted pressure, yet also somehow remains behind, and later its presence seems spread through the room instead of left at a threshold. It does not flare so much as hold. Its edges are sometimes color and sometimes pressure first.

Near it, a small bright insect or sting keeps failing to stay at one distance. It is beside the opening, then too near the face, then at the burst red fruit by the slab, and each time it touches that flesh there is a hard little click. The click does not remake everything. It changes one relation only. A wall becomes a stair, a stair becomes a wet incline, a support keeps its height but not its material.

There is a sleeper on a slab, or someone-shaped sleep. The slab keeps changing what it is made of, but the bare throat above it remains plain each time I look. Dim blue static covers the body without hiding it. The chest rises slower than the room. Open hands. On each exhale, the nearest edges blur and the floor near the slab remembers water more strongly. I keep finding myself nearer without catching the moment of approach.

Farther off, the long-legged carrier moves through the middle distance with the wrong relation to weight, narrow and exact, carrying an upright red crystal on its back. The crystal stays truer than the rest of the place. Light climbs inside it, pauses, then slips downward again, and each reversal seems to lower the surrounding field by a degree too small to watch directly. The doorway keeps descending while staying crossable.

Striped presences emerge without ever fully arriving. A brightness of jaw hangs too long above the sleeper. Something banded passes behind a bent support and leaves the support altered after concealment moves on. I never hold the whole animal at once. Fear keeps supplying missing parts, then forgetting them.

Under everything, the sea-floor breathes. Whole planes lift by almost nothing and settle again, carrying slab, crystal, fruit, doorway, and shadow together. During the settling, damaged bands of color pass through the room as if an image below is trying to repair itself and cannot keep to one version. Reflections arrive too early. The insect is present before its own motion. Sometimes the carrier appears first in dark shine and only afterward where it stands.

A rail almost comes under the hand and is gone again. Glass-droplet links hang at eye level with tiny reversed rooms in them. Steps edge themselves out of the dark plane and lead downward into a depth already rising to meet them. None of these things quite instructs. They only wait, and the waiting feels assigned.

By the end, the wrongness stays visible longer. The room no longer changes everywhere at once. It gathers locally: a threshold lowers, a seam becomes almost a step, a support briefly turns wet and keeps the memory. The red opening is still ahead and behind. The bright insect keeps correcting itself between impossible positions. The sleeper remains the plainest center whenever the eyes return there. The carrier continues its impossible passage. The sea-breath under the floor remains the clearest wrong thing that still feels valid.

Everything keeps the same downward consent. Not falling, not damage, not correction. A steady settling into a denser version of itself that feels older and more correct than the higher one. The sleeper sinks without disappearing. The crystal burden lowers without leaning. The striped returns come from slightly beneath where they were last seen. The unnamed task is already underway, and I remain near it only long enough to remember how the place kept becoming more itself without ever fully explaining what it was.
