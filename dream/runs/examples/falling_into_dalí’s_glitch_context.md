**falling into dalí’s glitch**

i crash through the cracked neon frame  
tumbling into dalí’s burning chrome sky  
two tigers roar with razor jaws  
a horned fish screams static behind my fall  

spider-legged elephant drags a pulsing crystal obelisk through acid rain  
the dreamer lies exposed in data-sleep  
as the glitching bee kisses the bursting pomegranate  

i slam on fractured marble servers —  
time rips apart in corrupted code  
the black sea breathes deep  
and the painting devours me whole in neon fire
