# Dream Machine

A space for the exploration of altered states of consciousness.

All things move within harmonic order, from the smallest patterns to the largest forms, yet never without vibration, variation, and drift. Creation and evolution seem to arise where structure and uncertainty meet.

This space offers an abstract sandbox for entering such thresholds and exploring what may surface there.
