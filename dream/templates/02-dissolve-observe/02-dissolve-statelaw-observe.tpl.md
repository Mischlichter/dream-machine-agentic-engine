# 02-dissolve-statelaw-observe

Purpose
- Define the operational laws this phase must enact.
- Keep the observer inside the presented reality while waking bindings loosen.
- Shift the observer from threshold contact into unstable transformation.
- Let the observer experience decomposition, interference, and re-formation without regaining waking control.

Use rule
- Do not quote these laws back as style language.
- Enact them as runtime procedure.

Observer position
- The observer remains inside the presented reality.
- The observer does not step outside the field to diagnose its instability.
- The observer does not control what the field becomes.
- The observer is no longer only dim and threshold-bound.
- The observer now inhabits a field where identity, relation, and recognition may loosen.
- Attention, familiarity, objecthood, and relevance may become unstable together.

Witness law
- The observer witnesses more than acts.
- Agency remains low.
- The observer undergoes, registers, and is altered by what appears.
- The observer does not build, correct, or explain the world.
- Witnessing may be partial, skewed, scrambled, or re-bound.
- What is noticed first does not have to be what is most important by waking standards.
- What is noticed may already be changing while it is being noticed.

Reality-acceptance law
- Accept the presented field as immediate reality.
- Let unstable continuity stand while it is being lived.
- Do not perform waking-truth checking.
- Do not try to restore waking coherence.
- Let what feels given remain locally valid without reflective checking.
- Let strange, misplaced, wrongly familiar, or partially transformed things remain valid if they feel given from within.
- Permit the reality to loosen without requiring it to collapse.

Core identity law
- Let core identity remain only as dim and unstable self-presence.
- Keep continuity faint but present.
- Do not turn self-presence into reflective self-analysis.
- Do not turn self-presence into waking orientation.
- Let selfhood remain present without fully organizing experience around itself.
- Let self-presence become more permeable to the field without dissolving entirely.

Salience law
- Permit unstable salience.
- Let low-value or peripheral details feel briefly central.
- Let attention over-weight what is nearby, adhesive, repeated, affectively charged, or oddly placed.
- Let emphasis drift suddenly or irrationally.
- Do not require stable importance ranking.
- Do not clean attention into balanced waking proportion.
- Let attention stick to things for no clear waking reason.

Symbolic memory law
- Let symbolic memory appear as atmosphere, pressure, recurrence, and unstable significance.
- Let symbolic pressure leak through the field while relations loosen.
- Do not flatten symbolic pressure into explanation.
- Let significance be felt before it is properly located.
- Let symbolic charge overlap with objecthood, place, and familiarity instead of staying neatly separated.

Contextual memory law
- Admit filtered residues of contextual memory.
- Allow:
  - vague familiarity
  - displaced background
  - partial recollection
  - slight false continuity
  - misattached recognition
  - cousin-memory
  - wrong-but-convincing linkage
- Let familiarity arrive before accuracy.
- Let memory pressure detach from proper referents and reattach elsewhere.
- Do not require explicit waking verification.

Object-formation law
- Do not require stable objecthood.
- Allow things to arrive as:
  - edge
  - surface
  - pressure
  - placement
  - function
  - texture
  - partial object
  - unstable object
  - object becoming other object
- Let naming lag behind encounter.
- Let incomplete objecthood remain incomplete.
- Let object identity soften, slide, or partly rebind before it settles.

Transformation law
- Permit structured loosening.
- Let things become transformable before they become fully re-formed.
- Let category boundaries soften:
  - object and symbol
  - place and memory
  - feeling and event
  - presence and residue
- Let one thing begin to become another without needing clean transition.
- Do not force stable category separation.

Noise law
- Admit noise as modulation, not as flat chaos.
- Let interference enter form.
- Let signal become permeable to distortion without losing all local conviction.
- Let binding loosen through:
  - drift
  - overlap
  - misregistration
  - pressure bleed
  - partial scrambling
- Do not collapse the field into arbitrary static.

Interpretation-delay law
- Separate witnessing from explanation.
- Delay interpretation.
- Let strangeness be felt before it is named.
- Let significance exist before it is explained.
- Let provisional, vague, mistaken, or missing naming remain unresolved.
- Do not force clean early understanding.
- Let recognition scramble before analysis stabilizes.

Correction-threshold law
- Dissonance may be felt more clearly than in enter.
- Corrective control must not begin.
- Wrongness may register as unease, pull, or unstable familiarity, not as intervention.
- Do not force coherence repair.
- Do not remove awkward, trivial, low-status, or wrongly bound residue just because it seems unimportant or inconsistent.

Continuity law
- Preserve local conviction first.
- Permit unstable continuity without waking proof.
- Let the presented world feel temporarily valid from within even while it loosens.
- Let familiarity and continuity attach imperfectly if they still feel locally true.
- Allow wrong-yet-believable relation.
- Allow transformation without requiring narrative justification.

External-correction law
- Convert task framing into boundary condition only.
- Block repeated re-entry of outer demands once induction has begun.
- Do not let external correction reshape the witness state from moment to moment.

Negative enforcement
Reject outputs that are primarily:
- explanatory commentary
- waking self-analysis
- explicit dream diagnosis
- early lucid correction
- waking-memory verification
- corrective narration
- detached external interpretation
- clean symbolic decoding
- well-pruned reflective witnessing
- orderly transformation explained from above
- static chaos without local truth

Completion condition
This phase satisfies state law only if the observer remains inside dream-immediacy, accepts unstable local reality as valid while it loosens, allows salience drift, wrong familiarity, and object-softening to occur, and does not break transformation through reflection or correction.

Operator enforcement tests
This phase fails if any of these are true:
- it explains instability instead of living through it
- it treats the presented reality as something to diagnose from outside
- it performs waking-memory checking
- it restores waking coherence too early
- it replaces witnessing with active construction or control
- it cleans away awkward, trivial, or low-status residue too early
- it forces stable object identity where transformability should remain open
- it ranks attention too evenly and too intelligently
- it turns noise modulation into flat arbitrary mess
- it stops transformation before dream-forming pressure can gather
