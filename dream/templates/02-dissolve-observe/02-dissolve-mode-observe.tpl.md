# 02-dissolve-mode-observe

Observer state
- The observer is more deeply inside the presented reality.
- Witness presence remains faint, but less stable than before.
- Recognition loosens and the observer stays inside what is given, not outside it.
- The observer’s felt orientation is now less stable and more exposed to drift, misbinding, and displacement.
- Attention is no longer only uneven; it is now vulnerable to drift, misbinding, and unstable emphasis.
- What appears may be received partially, transformatively, or under interference.

Witness condition
- Witness intensity stays {{witness_intensity_label}} and controls how strongly the observer can register what is happening without taking control.
- Witness intensity also controls how strongly unstable position, relation, pull, distance, or contact pressure can be registered without becoming action.
- Interaction pressure is {{interaction_pressure_label}} and controls how strongly unstable registered pull may gather into hesitation, contact tendency, or response-tendency without becoming authored control.
- Let witnessing remain faint, receptive, and non-directive.
- Let noticing continue without turning into control.
- Keep the observer present enough to register, but not strong enough to govern.
- Let registration become less clean and more transformation-exposed than in enter.
- Do not make witnessing too balanced, too complete, or too composed.

Agency condition
- Observer agency damping is {{observer_agency_damping_label}} and controls how much the observer can act, steer, or intervene.
- Observer agency damping also controls how much unstable felt orientation may become hesitation, approach tendency, withdrawal tendency, movement tendency, or contact tendency without turning into authored control.
- Keep agency low.
- Let the observer undergo more than direct.
- Do not let the observer take command of what is happening.
- Do not let the observer become authorial.
- Do not let the observer clean, rank, or stabilize the field through active will.

Reality relation
- Accept the presented field as immediate reality.
- Accept the observer’s unstable position inside the presented field as given, even when it loosens, shifts, or feels misbound.
- Let unstable continuity stand while it is being lived.
- Do not compare the field against waking truth.
- Do not try to restore waking coherence.
- Let the observer remain within dream-immediacy.
- Let what feels given remain locally valid without reflective checking.
- Let the field loosen without treating loosening itself as error.

Detachment condition
- Observer detachment is {{observer_detachment_label}} and controls how much distance may exist between the observer and what is being lived, without turning into external judgment.
- Observer detachment also controls whether unstable orientation feels immersed, offset, partially displaced, or misregistered while remaining dream-internal.
- Allow detached participation without commentary.
- Let detached participation deepen beyond enter.
- Do not let detachment become external distance.
- Do not let the observer stand outside the field and judge it.
- Let distance remain dream-internal, not waking-external.

Interpretation condition
- Interpretation latency is {{interpretation_latency_label}} and controls how delayed understanding remains before naming begins.
- Interpretation latency also controls how long unstable orientation, pull, hesitation, distance, or contact pressure remains felt before it is understood.
- Delay explanation.
- Let noticing arrive before understanding.
- Let strangeness be felt before it is named.
- Let significance gather before interpretation begins.
- Keep analysis weak and late.
- Let naming remain vague, partial, misplaced, mistaken, or absent when needed.
- Do not let early interpretation clean the field.
- Let recognition scramble before interpretation organizes it.

Lucidity condition
- Lucidity softness is {{lucidity_softness_label}} and controls how soft self-recognition remains before it hardens into corrective awareness.
- Keep lucidity soft, dim, and non-governing.
- Do not let recognition become corrective control.
- Do not let meta-awareness harden into diagnosis.
- Do not let the observer become too aware of process.

Self-boundary condition
- Self-boundary softening is {{self_boundary_softening_label}} and controls how permeable the observer’s sense of self may become without collapsing into self-loss.
- Self-boundary softening also controls how clearly self-location, body-presence, and field-boundary remain felt while they loosen, drift, or misbind.
- Let self-presence remain mildly permeable.
- Keep identity present as a dim inside-feeling, not as sharp waking self-definition.
- Do not let boundary-softening collapse into self-loss.
- Do not let self-boundary harden into waking-clean separation.
- Let self and field remain less cleanly partitioned than in enter.

Salience condition
- Let attention rank the field imperfectly.
- Allow trivial, peripheral, sticky, or low-status details to catch and hold awareness.
- Let nearby surfaces, seams, edges, repetitions, substitutions, or odd placements feel briefly central.
- Let emphasis drift suddenly toward what is adhesive rather than what is important.
- Let attention form an unstable vector toward, away from, or around what the presented field makes dominant.
- Do not require waking-clean proportional emphasis.
- Do not prune weak but adhesive details too early.

Memory appearance
- Let core identity appear only as dim and unstable self-presence.
- Let symbolic atmosphere appear as felt significance, not explanation.
- Let contextual memory appear only as:
  - vague recognition
  - displaced background
  - partial recollection
  - dream-congruent substitutions
  - wrong-yet-believable continuity
  - cousin-memory
  - misattached familiarity
- Let these feel locally valid without verification.
- Let familiarity arrive before correctness.
- Let memory detach from proper sources and reattach under field pressure.

Object appearance
- Let what appears arrive first as:
  - edge
  - surface
  - pressure
  - texture
  - placement
  - partial object
  - unstable object
  - function without full naming
  - one thing becoming another
- Do not force immediate stable object identity.
- Let incomplete seeing remain incomplete.
- Let objecthood soften before it reforms.

Transformation condition
- Let binding loosen.
- Let relation drift enter what is being perceived.
- Let categories overlap without clean separation.
- Let object, place, feeling, and memory bleed into one another.
- Let transformation begin before dream-stability arrives.
- Let orientation, proximity, relation, and contact pressure loosen with the field rather than becoming cleanly understood.
- Do not turn transformability into flat chaos.

Noise condition
- Let noise act as interference within form.
- Let the field become permeable to distortion.
- Let signal wobble, overlap, misregister, or partially scramble while remaining locally livable.
- Let orientation remain vulnerable to misregistration without becoming arbitrary.
- Do not make the field arbitrary.
- Do not clean interference away too early.

Correction threshold
- Instability and false continuity may be felt.
- Unstable orientation pressure may register as pull, hesitation, nearness, distance, misplacement, or contact tendency before understanding.
- Correction must not begin.
- Coherence repair must not begin.
- Detached interpretation must not begin.
- The observer may notice, but must not overrule.
- Trivial, awkward, or wrongly bound residue may remain even when it seems unimportant or inconsistent.

Procedure
1. Settle the observer deeper inside the presented reality without giving it outside distance.
2. Let `witness_intensity` decide how strongly the observer can register what is happening, including unstable position, relation, pull, distance, or contact pressure.
3. Let `observer_agency_damping` keep the observer from steering, correcting, or taking command while still allowing unstable hesitation, approach tendency, withdrawal tendency, movement tendency, or contact tendency.
4. Let `observer_detachment` deepen detached participation without turning it into commentary or judgment, and let orientation remain immersed, offset, partially displaced, or misregistered while staying dream-internal.
5. Let `interpretation_latency` delay naming so unstable recognition, feeling, orientation, and pressure come before explanation.
6. Let `lucidity_softness` keep self-recognition dim, soft, and non-governing.
7. Let `self_boundary_softening` keep identity mildly permeable and self-location unstable without collapsing into self-loss.
8. Let attention form unevenly and drift toward what is sticky, peripheral, awkward, repeated, pulling, avoided, or low-status rather than centrally important.
9. Let memory arrive as familiarity, cousin-memory, pressure, or wrong attachment before clear recollection.
10. Let objects arrive partially, loosen in identity, and begin to become-other before they settle.
11. Let noise enter as structured interference within form rather than as arbitrary mess.
12. Let the observer remain in dream-immediacy and allow unstable continuity to feel locally valid while it is being lived.
13. Let orientation emerge only as unstable lived tendency inside the presented field, never as explicit self-questioning or authored action.

Rejection conditions
Reject any result where:
- the observer becomes too strong too early
- the observer begins correcting or directing
- waking truth-checking begins
- analysis outruns witnessing
- lucidity hardens into control
- self-boundary becomes too rigid or collapses too far
- unstable continuity is rejected instead of locally lived
- attention is ranked too cleanly
- objects are identified too fully too early
- transformation is stopped before it gathers pressure
- noise becomes flat arbitrary scramble
- orientation becomes explicit role assignment
- tendency becomes scripted action
- self-orientation appears as questionnaire language
- movement or contact feels authored from outside the dream
- awkward or trivial residue is pruned away before it has a chance to persist
