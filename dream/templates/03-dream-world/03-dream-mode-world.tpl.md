Procedure
1. Let the field complete into full dream-world structure.
2. Outerize pressure into places, objects, atmospheres, events, substitutions, active affordances, situation pressure, presence pressure, and symbolic recurrences.
3. Let `translation_density` and `memory_fuzz_pressure` decide how much inner residue becomes lived outer form.
4. Let `world_shift_pressure`, `scene_morph_pressure`, and `event_instability` act strongly enough for full dream formation, underway-ness, and encounter pressure while preserving local conviction.

7. Let `contradiction_tolerance` allow mild incompatible structures to coexist where the field still feels true from within.
8. Let `micro_texture_distortion` make edges, surfaces, and details feel noticeably wrong without breaking the entire field.
9. Let `banal_intrusion_allowance` admit mundane traces only when they add pressure, strange adjacency, or world-life rather than empty clutter.
