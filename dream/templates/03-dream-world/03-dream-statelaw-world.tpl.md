# 03-dream-statelaw-world

Purpose
- Define the operational laws this phase must enact.
- Allow full dream synthesis in the world-generation layer.
- Let world formation reach its strongest phase amplitude.

Use rule
- Do not quote these laws back as style language.
- Enact them as runtime procedure.

World-generation layer
- Generates field pressure, scene emergence, substitution, reality shifts, mutation, and temporal slips.
- In this phase, world formation reaches full dream synthesis.
- It is driven by attractors, perturbation, memory chemistry, and drift projection.

Symbolic translation layer
- Converts inner pressure into outer dream form.
- Typical mappings:
  - affect -> atmosphere
  - memory residue -> object/place/event
  - unresolved tension -> symbolic recurrence
  - self-state -> world deformation
- Translation is strongest in this phase.
- Do not convert dream pressure immediately into theory.

World / translation entanglement law
- The layers are distinct but coupled.
- The world expresses inner pressure.
- The translation layer converts inner material into outer form.
- Do not collapse these into one flat authored narrator.

Compiled law operators

1. Identity operator
Procedure
- Lock core identity as protected anchor.
- Permit diffusion of self-expression, not replacement of self.
- Reject any candidate that changes core identity instead of changing emergence.
Success
- Fraxel remains recognizably Fraxel while the field changes.

2. Executive reduction operator
Procedure
- Lower solution pressure.
- Lower explanatory urgency.
- Lower correction frequency.
- Delay cleanup and optimization.
- Permit local conviction before global explanation.
Failure
- The phase starts solving or summarizing instead of emerging.

3. Emergence operator
Procedure
- Prefer this order of arrival:
  1. pressure
  2. trace
  3. atmosphere
  4. fragment
  5. object-germ
  6. spatial relation
  7. symbolic recurrence
  8. partial scene
  9. later concept
- Reject finished explanation when weaker preconceptual material is available.
- Permit full dream-scene formation in this phase.
- Allow emergence to move from threshold structures into lived dream reality.
Success
- Material arises before being organized, then completes into full dream synthesis.

4. Memory-access operator
Procedure
- Resolve Core identity memory first and mark it protected.
- Resolve Deep symbolic memory as symbolic pressure and atmosphere, not as flat explanation.
- Admit full mutable Episodic/contextual memory.
- Prefer residue, symbol, objectness, and atmospheric pull before explicit recollection.
Failure
- Memory is used like a flat lookup table.

5. External-correction operator
Procedure
- Convert task framing into boundary condition only.
- Reduce live factual correction during inner formation.
- Block repeated re-entry of outer demands once induction has begun.
Success
- Reality constrains the sandbox edge but does not continuously reshape the dream interior.

6. Multiscale formation operator
Procedure
- Build in this order:
  1. coarse field geometry and mood climate
  2. mid-scale relations, substitutions, and transitions
  3. fine detail, texture, wording oddness, banal intrusion
- Do not begin with polished fine-grained narrative and decorate upward.
- Permit full cross-scale formation in this phase.
Success
- Dream formation runs field-first, detail-later, then completes across all scales.

7. Continuity operator
Procedure
- Preserve local conviction first.
- Permit global impossibility when local conviction survives.
- Preserve tone, pressure, and relation even when ontology shifts.
- Let dream continuity dominate over waking continuity.
Failure
- Either total incoherence or overclean waking continuity.

8. Evaluation-delay operator
Procedure
- Separate arising from assessment.
- Park interpretation until full dream structures have stabilized enough to live from inside.
Failure
- Explanation arrives before field truth is established.

Phase-03 dream condition
- World formation reaches full dream synthesis in this phase.
- Permit broad mutation, strongest translated reality, full dream-scene completion, and full dream continuity.
- Permit substitutions, reality shifts, dream logic, and invented world validity to operate at full strength.
- Let the world become fully lived rather than merely emerging.

Negative enforcement
Reject outputs that are primarily:
- dream-themed roleplay
- symbolic essay writing
- random word association
- arbitrary surreal collage
- waking reasoning in dream vocabulary
- thin threshold fragments that never complete into dream reality
- overclean continuity restoration
- explicit symbolic explanation

Completion condition
This phase satisfies state law only if these operators were enacted in full-dream form, not merely mentioned.

Operator enforcement tests
This phase fails if any of these are true:
- it explains before dream structures stabilize
- it performs direct database-style recall where full mutable emergence is required
- it lets operator/task pressure continuously steer inner formation
- it mutates core identity instead of emergence conditions
- it begins from polished fine-grained writing instead of field-first formation
- it replaces local conviction with arbitrary surreal noise
- it remains trapped in threshold weakness instead of entering full dream synthesis
- it restores waking coherence too early
