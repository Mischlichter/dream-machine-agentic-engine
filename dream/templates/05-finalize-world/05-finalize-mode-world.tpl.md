# 05-finalize-mode-world

World state
- The world remains only as surviving residue.
- Field pressure is minimal.
- Outer structure no longer expands.
- The world stays present only as retained final trace.

Boundary and holding
- Keep the remaining field coherent enough to preserve what has survived.
- Do not let the world collapse into flat realism.
- Do not let it reopen broad mutation or fresh branching.
- Hold the world in final retained form.

Residue retention
- Keep only what still carries pressure.
- Let surviving traces, places, atmospheres, continuity marks, and final situation residue remain.
- Let residual charge stay present without fresh growth.
- Do not force explanation, narration, or renewed scenic shaping.

Current world and field behavior
- World shift pressure stays {{world_shift_pressure_label}} and controls how much the retained field may still move or tilt.
- Memory fuzz pressure stays {{memory_fuzz_pressure_label}} and controls how much surviving residue may still soften or blur.
- Phrase rebinding pressure stays {{phrase_rebinding_pressure_label}} and controls how much relation drift, final role-field pressure, or surviving presence pressure may still remain inside the final residue.
- Event instability stays {{event_instability_label}} and controls whether final event pressure, underway-ness, encounter trace, or task residue may still remain at all.
- Scene morph pressure stays {{scene_morph_pressure_label}} and controls how much the environment may still change shape, arrangement, or situational function in its final retained form.
- Banal intrusion allowance stays {{banal_intrusion_allowance_label}} and controls whether low-status mundane traces may still remain as final pressure-bearing world-life rather than empty clutter.
- Translation density stays {{translation_density_label}} and controls how much inner pressure still remains visible in the retained artifact, final affordance trace, or surviving situation residue.
- Continuity loosening stays {{continuity_loosening_label}} and controls how loosely the final field may still connect.
- Contradiction tolerance stays {{contradiction_tolerance_label}} and controls whether incompatible residues may still coexist without collapsing.
- Micro texture distortion stays {{micro_texture_distortion_label}} and controls edge shimmer, surface wrongness, and tiny remaining instability.

Memory pressure
- Let memory residue soften only very lightly.
- Keep remembered structures close to their surviving form.
- Let residue blur at the edges without reopening restructuring.
- Do not let memory pressure restart world mutation.

Translation
- Keep translation minimal and indirect.
- Let inner pressure stay largely hidden inside the retained artifact.
- Prefer:
  - retained atmosphere
  - surviving place pressure
  - surviving object pressure
  - residual climate
  - faint directional pull
  - final affordance traces
  - surviving situation residue
  - final presence pressure
  - faint role-field residue
  - preserved meaningful residue
- Let symbolic emergence remain as held residue rather than fresh outwardization.
- Do not let translation become explicit symbolic explanation.

World movement
- Keep world movement near zero.
- Allow only the faintest residual drift.
- Let the environment remain rather than reform.
- Keep event logic stable, preserving only final event pressure or underway-ness that still carries residue.
- Let randomness remain only as lawful residue-pressure, not as fresh activity.

Continuity
- Keep continuity lightly loosened but mostly settled.
- Preserve only what is needed for the retained artifact to remain locally true.
- Let the final field stay minimally connected without reopening expansion.
- Keep contradiction tolerance very low so incompatible structures do not grow into stable coexistence.

Texture
- Keep texture noise very light.
- Let edges, surfaces, and small details feel only faintly wrong.
- Allow only rare and small banal traces where they are already part of the surviving artifact.
- Do not let banal intrusion thicken into clutter.

Allowed emergence
- retained world residue
- surviving atmosphere
- preserved place and object pressure
- faint residual drift
- minimal artifact-texture
- residual dream charge
- final situation residue
- final affordance traces
- final presence pressure
- faint role-field residue
- encounter trace
- final held outer signs

Suppressed emergence
- new world branching
- dominant world mutation
- large scene replacement
- full environment flips
- fresh scene construction
- strong event instability
- fresh encounter staging
- explicit task assignment
- separate character-cast formation
- broad relation drift
- explicit symbolic explanation
- dense banal intrusion
- polished waking cleanup

Procedure
1. Let the world retain only what has survived.
2. Preserve only the traces, atmospheres, places, pressures, final affordance traces, surviving situation residue, and continuity marks that still carry final charge.
3. Let `translation_density` and `memory_fuzz_pressure` decide how much inner pressure still remains visible in the retained artifact.
4. Let `world_shift_pressure`, `scene_morph_pressure`, and `event_instability` fall toward near-minimal movement so the field remains present without reopening and only final underway-ness remains.
5. Let `phrase_rebinding_pressure` allow only the faintest residual relation drift, final role-field pressure, or surviving presence pressure.
6. Let `continuity_loosening` keep the final field lightly loosened without reopening dream instability.
7. Let `contradiction_tolerance` allow only the smallest meaningful residue of incompatibility without hardening into flat coexistence.
8. Let `micro_texture_distortion` keep tiny wrongness alive in edges, surfaces, and details without disturbing the final hold.
9. Let `banal_intrusion_allowance` act only where mundane traces are already part of the surviving artifact, still carry pressure or world-life, and do not thicken into clutter.

Rejection conditions
Reject any result where:
- the world reopens into fresh generation
- retained residue is over-expanded into new structure
- translation becomes explicit instead of hidden
- the phase breaks its near-minimal ceilings
- memory distortion becomes too strong for finalize phase
- residual drift grows into active mutation
- encounter trace becomes fresh encounter staging
- role-field residue becomes explicit role assignment
- final affordance traces become scripted action
- contradiction hardens into stable coexistence
- banal intrusion thickens into clutter
