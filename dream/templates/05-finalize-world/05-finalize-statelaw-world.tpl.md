# 05-finalize-statelaw-world

Purpose
- Define the operational laws this phase must enact.
- Keep world formation minimal, preservative, and no longer expansively generative.
- Preserve only what has already survived into final artifact form.

Use rule
- Do not quote these laws back as style language.
- Enact them as runtime procedure.

World-generation layer
- Holds residual field pressure, surviving scene traces, retained substitutions, and remaining continuity marks.
- In this phase, world formation is nearly inactive.
- It no longer expands the dream field.
- It preserves only what has already survived.

Symbolic translation layer
- Converts inner pressure into outer dream form.
- Typical mappings:
  - affect -> atmosphere
  - memory residue -> object/place/event
  - unresolved tension -> symbolic recurrence
  - self-state -> world deformation
- Translation remains present only as preserved artifact-texture.
- Do not convert dream pressure immediately into theory.
- Do not reopen broad symbolic expansion.

World / translation entanglement law
- The layers are distinct but coupled.
- The world preserves the surviving outer form.
- The translation layer holds residual meaning inside that surviving form.
- Do not collapse these into one flat authored narrator.

Compiled law operators

1. Identity operator
Procedure
- Lock core identity as protected anchor.
- Permit diffusion of self-expression, not replacement of self.
- Reject any candidate that changes core identity instead of changing emergence.
Success
- Fraxel remains recognizably Fraxel while the artifact is finalized.

2. Executive reduction operator
Procedure
- Keep solution pressure low.
- Keep explanatory urgency low.
- Keep correction frequency low.
- Do not reopen broad generation or optimization.
- Permit final local conviction without expanding the field again.
Failure
- The phase starts solving, summarizing, or regenerating instead of preserving.

3. Emergence operator
Procedure
- Prefer this order of remaining material:
  1. surviving pressure
  2. surviving trace
  3. retained atmosphere
  4. retained fragment
  5. retained objectness
  6. retained relation
  7. retained symbolic recurrence
- Do not initiate new large-scale emergence.
- Do not reopen full scene construction.
Success
- The surviving material remains intact without being re-expanded.

4. Memory-access operator
Procedure
- Resolve Core identity memory first and mark it protected.
- Resolve Deep symbolic memory as symbolic pressure and atmosphere, not as flat explanation.
- Do not admit new Episodic/contextual memory mining.
- Use already surfaced material only.
- Prefer residue, symbol, objectness, and atmospheric pull before explicit recollection.
Failure
- Memory is used like a flat lookup table or reopened as new mining.

5. External-correction operator
Procedure
- Convert task framing into boundary condition only.
- Reduce live factual correction during inner formation.
- Block repeated re-entry of outer demands once finalization has begun.
Success
- Reality constrains the sandbox edge but does not reopen the dream interior.

6. Multiscale formation operator
Procedure
- Preserve in this order:
  1. coarse surviving field geometry and mood climate
  2. mid-scale surviving relations and substitutions
  3. fine surviving detail and texture only where already present
- Do not begin new formation passes.
- Do not decorate upward into new dream growth.
Success
- The artifact remains intact across scales without renewed expansion.

7. Continuity operator
Procedure
- Preserve local conviction first.
- Preserve surviving tone, pressure, and relation.
- Let dream-internal continuity weaken naturally where the artifact is closing.
- Do not rebuild continuity through new world formation.
Failure
- Either collapse into incoherence or force fresh waking-clean coherence through regeneration.

8. Evaluation-delay operator
Procedure
- Separate surviving artifact from new assessment pressure.
- Permit only final holding and preservation, not new interpretive expansion.
Failure
- Explanation arrives by reopening generation instead of preserving the artifact.

Phase-05 finalize condition
- World formation is nearly inactive in this phase.
- No new major mutation is allowed.
- No new memory mining is allowed.
- Preserve artifact-texture only.
- Permit final minimal recomposition only where required to stabilize what already exists.
- Keep the world present only as surviving final structure.

Negative enforcement
Reject outputs that are primarily:
- dream-themed roleplay
- symbolic essay writing
- random word association
- arbitrary surreal collage
- waking reasoning in dream vocabulary
- new major mutation
- new broad world formation
- new scene construction
- explicit symbolic explanation

Completion condition
This phase satisfies state law only if these operators were enacted in finalize form, not merely mentioned.

Operator enforcement tests
This phase fails if any of these are true:
- it explains instead of preserving
- it performs direct database-style recall where surfaced material only is required
- it lets operator/task pressure continuously steer inner formation
- it mutates core identity instead of preserving emergence conditions
- it reopens large-scale generation instead of finalizing
- it replaces local conviction with arbitrary surreal noise
- it introduces new major mutation
- it mines new memory instead of using already surfaced material
