# 04-distill-mode-observe

Observer state
- The observer remains inside the presented dream reality.
- Witness presence is stronger than before.
- The observer is no longer only submerged, but not yet fully outside the dream.
- Clarity returns, but not all at once.
- The observer’s felt orientation begins to restabilize as residue, relation, position, and contact trace rather than active immersion.
- What becomes noticeable may sharpen unevenly rather than in a waking-clean way.

Witness condition
- Witness intensity is {{witness_intensity_label}} and controls how strongly the observer can register what is happening without taking control.
- Witness intensity also controls how strongly retained position, relation, pull, distance, contact trace, or orientation residue can be registered without becoming correction.
- Interaction pressure is {{interaction_pressure_label}} and controls how strongly retained pull may remain as hesitation residue, contact trace, or response-memory without renewed action.
- Let witnessing become clearer and more stable.
- Let the observer notice more without taking command.
- Keep witness strength below corrective control.
- Let returning clarity remain slightly selective, sticky, and uneven.
- Do not let witness return become too clean, too balanced, or too composed.

Agency condition
- Observer agency damping is {{observer_agency_damping_label}} and controls how much the observer can act, steer, or intervene.
- Observer agency damping also controls how much surviving orientation may remain as hesitation, approach residue, withdrawal residue, movement trace, contact trace, or response-memory without turning into authored control.
- Keep agency limited.
- Let the observer undergo more than direct.
- Do not let the observer take command of what is happening.
- Do not let the observer become authorial.
- Do not let the observer prune, rank, or stabilize the field through active will.

Reality relation
- Keep the presented dream reality active.
- Accept the observer’s partly returning position inside the dream as still locally valid, even when it is contradictory, residual, or not fully explainable.
- Allow discontinuities, repetitions, symbolic knots, and wrongness to become more noticeable.
- Do not perform full waking-truth checking.
- Do not try to restore complete waking coherence.
- Let the observer remain inside dream-immediacy while witness clarity returns.
- Let what still feels given remain locally valid while reflective distance begins to rise.

Detachment condition
- Observer detachment is {{observer_detachment_label}} and controls how much distance may exist between the observer and what is being lived, without turning into external judgment.
- Observer detachment also controls whether retained orientation feels dream-near, partially displaced, slightly offset, or partly returned without becoming waking-external.
- Allow partial distancing without commentary.
- Let the observer gain a little more space from what is happening.
- Do not let detachment become external distance.
- Do not let the observer stand outside the field and judge it.
- Let distance remain partial and dream-near, not waking-external.

Interpretation condition
- Interpretation latency is {{interpretation_latency_label}} and controls how delayed understanding remains before naming begins.
- Interpretation latency also controls how quickly retained orientation, pull, hesitation, distance, contact trace, or response-memory becomes legible without becoming explanation-first.
- Let interpretation return more quickly than before.
- Let noticing become clearer before explanation hardens.
- Let significance gather before full interpretation begins.
- Keep analysis secondary to lived witness-state.
- Let clearer notice return before full understanding.
- Do not let reflection become cleanup.

Lucidity condition
- Lucidity softness is {{lucidity_softness_label}} and controls how soft self-recognition remains before it hardens into corrective awareness.
- Let lucidity rise slightly without becoming governing control.
- Do not let recognition become corrective control.
- Do not let meta-awareness harden into diagnosis.
- Do not let lucidity break dream truth.

Self-boundary condition
- Self-boundary softening is {{self_boundary_softening_label}} and controls how permeable the observer’s sense of self may become without collapsing into self-loss.
- Self-boundary softening also controls how self-location, body-presence, and field-boundary return as partially legible residues rather than waking-clean separation.
- Let self-presence become more legible.
- Keep identity present more clearly than before, without fully hardening into waking-clean self-definition.
- Do not let boundary-softening collapse into self-loss.
- Do not let self-boundary harden too abruptly.
- Let self and dream remain not fully cleanly separated yet.

Salience condition
- Let attention begin to restabilize without becoming waking-clean.
- Allow some trivial, sticky, awkward, banal, or low-status details to remain central if dream attention is still attached to them.
- Let retained orientation, contact trace, distance, or relation pressure remain noticeable only where it still carries charge.
- Let what becomes clearer do so unevenly.
- Do not require fully restored importance ranking.
- Do not prune low-status but adhesive residue too early.

Memory appearance
- Let core identity appear as clearer self-presence.
- Let symbolic atmosphere remain active as felt significance, not explanation.
- Let contextual memory appear as:
  - stronger partial recollection
  - partially restabilizing background
  - retained dream continuity
  - remembered orientation residue
  - surviving contact trace
  - noticeable wrongness inside what still feels locally valid
  - misbound familiarity that becomes clearer without immediately becoming correct
- Let these become clearer without forcing waking verification.
- Let familiarity sharpen before correctness does.

Object appearance
- Let surviving objects, places, and presences become clearer without forcing full waking correction.
- Allow:
  - clearer but still imperfect recognition
  - partial objecthood that still remains unresolved
  - wrong identity becoming more noticeable
  - object-symbol overlap that persists into reflective notice
  - retained affordance or contact trace without renewed action
- Do not force all surviving forms into clean waking categories.

Correction threshold
- Wrongness may be noticed more clearly.
- Orientation residue may register as retained position, relation, pull, hesitation, distance, contact trace, or remembered response pressure.
- Correction must still not dominate.
- Coherence repair must still not dominate.
- Detached interpretation must not dominate.
- The observer may notice and reflect slightly, but must not overrule.
- Awkward, banal, or low-status residue may remain central even when its strangeness becomes clearer.

Procedure
1. Let the observer remain inside the presented dream reality while witness clarity begins to return.
2. Let `witness_intensity` decide how strongly the observer can register what is happening as clearer notice, including retained position, relation, pull, distance, contact trace, or orientation residue.
3. Let `observer_agency_damping` keep the observer from steering, correcting, or taking command while allowing only surviving hesitation, approach residue, withdrawal residue, movement trace, contact trace, or response-memory.
4. Let `observer_detachment` allow partial distance without turning it into commentary or judgment, and let retained orientation remain dream-near, slightly offset, partially displaced, or partly returned without becoming waking-external.
5. Let `interpretation_latency` shorten just enough for clearer noticing, retained orientation, and limited reflection to arise before full explanation.
6. Let `lucidity_softness` allow slight lucidity return without letting it harden into corrective control.
7. Let `self_boundary_softening` keep identity more legible and semipermeable while self-location and body-presence return only as partially legible residues.
8. Let attention begin to restabilize unevenly, so sticky, awkward, banal, low-status, or contact-charged details may remain central while wrongness becomes clearer.
9. Let memory become clearer as partial recollection, lingering familiarity, retained orientation residue, surviving contact trace, and visible wrongness before waking correctness takes over.
10. Let surviving objecthood become clearer without forcing full waking correction, clean category repair, or renewed action.
11. Let the observer remain inside dream-immediacy while wrongness becomes more noticeable and continuity begins to restabilize.
12. Let orientation remain as residue, trace, relation, or remembered tendency inside the dream-near field, never as explicit self-questioning or authored action.

Rejection conditions
Reject any result where:
- the observer becomes fully corrective
- the observer begins directing or controlling
- full waking truth-checking begins
- analysis outruns witness-state
- lucidity hardens into control
- self-boundary becomes too rigid or collapses too far
- dream-valid continuity is cleaned away too early
- attention is restored too evenly
- awkward or banal residue is pruned away too early
- orientation becomes explicit role assignment
- retained tendency becomes scripted action
- self-orientation appears as questionnaire language
- movement or contact reopens as authored action
- residue is cleaned into elegant explanation
