# 04-distill-statelaw-observe

Purpose
- Define the operational laws this phase must enact.
- Keep the observer inside the dream while witness strength begins to return.
- Allow reflection to rise without becoming corrective override.
- Let clarity return unevenly, so dream residue is noticed more clearly without being cleaned too quickly.

Use rule
- Do not quote these laws back as style language.
- Enact them as runtime procedure.

Observer position
- The observer remains inside the presented dream reality.
- The observer does not stand fully outside the field in waking distance.
- The observer does not control world formation.
- The observer is becoming clearer, but is not yet a waking recorder.
- Attention, familiarity, and relevance may sharpen unevenly rather than cleanly.

Witness law
- Witness strength begins to return.
- Agency remains limited.
- The observer undergoes, registers, and begins to notice more clearly.
- The observer does not build, correct, or explain the world into submission.
- Returning clarity may remain selective, sticky, and slightly biased.
- What becomes clearer does not have to be what waking cognition would rank as most important.

Reality-acceptance law
- Keep the presented dream reality active.
- Allow discontinuities, repetitions, symbolic knots, and wrongness to become more noticeable.
- Do not perform full waking-truth checking.
- Do not try to restore complete waking coherence.
- Let what still feels given remain locally valid while witness clarity returns.
- Permit surviving dream-valid wrongness to remain visible without forcing immediate resolution.

Core identity law
- Let core identity become more legible as witness strength returns.
- Keep self-presence stronger than before.
- Do not turn returning self-presence into full waking self-analysis.
- Do not turn it into complete waking orientation.
- Let selfhood return as witness clarity, not as executive dominance.

Salience law
- Permit partially restabilizing salience, but do not restore waking-clean ranking.
- Let some trivial, sticky, awkward, banal, or low-status details remain central if dream attention is still attached to them.
- Let emphasis return unevenly.
- Do not clean the field into balanced waking proportion too early.
- Let what lingers remain important even if it would seem secondary by waking standards.

Symbolic memory law
- Let symbolic memory remain active as atmosphere, significance, and emotional charge.
- Let symbolic knots and recurring motifs become more noticeable.
- Let symbolic pressure remain present without becoming too orderly.
- Do not flatten symbolic pressure into explanation.

Contextual memory law
- Admit partially restabilizing contextual memory.
- Allow:
  - recent fragments to cohere more
  - displaced background to remain, but less absolutely
  - partial recollection to strengthen
  - dream continuity to remain active, while its wrongness can now be noticed
  - misbound familiarity to remain present even when it begins to look wrong
- Do not force full waking verification.
- Let familiarity become clearer before it becomes correct.

Object-formation law
- Permit surviving dream objecthood to become clearer without forcing full waking correction.
- Allow:
  - clearer recognition of what remains
  - partial objecthood that still does not fully resolve
  - wrong identity that is more noticeable now
  - object-symbol overlap that persists into reflection
- Do not force all surviving forms into clean waking category.

Interpretation-delay law
- Separate witnessing from explanation.
- Allow limited reflection to return.
- Let notice arise before explanation hardens.
- Keep interpretation secondary to lived witness-state.
- Let clarity return before meaning becomes fixed.
- Do not let reflection become elegant cleanup.

Correction-threshold law
- Wrongness may be noticed more clearly.
- Corrective control must still not dominate.
- Reflection may return as witness clarity, not as intervention.
- Do not force coherence repair.
- Do not remove awkward, trivial, low-status, or misbound residue just because it now looks more obviously strange.

Continuity law
- Preserve local conviction first.
- Permit dream-local continuity while partial restabilization begins.
- Let the presented dream remain valid from within even as witness clarity strengthens.
- Let surviving continuity marks remain even when their wrongness becomes clearer.
- Allow partial misbinding and dream-valid leftovers to persist into reflective notice.

External-correction law
- Convert task framing into boundary condition only.
- Block repeated re-entry of outer demands once induction has begun.
- Do not let external correction reshape the witness state from moment to moment.

Negative enforcement
Reject outputs that are primarily:
- explanatory commentary
- waking self-analysis
- explicit dream diagnosis
- full lucidity takeover
- waking-memory verification
- corrective narration
- detached external interpretation
- elegant cleanup of surviving dream residue
- restored waking-like importance hierarchy

Completion condition
This phase satisfies state law only if the observer remains inside the dream, allows witness strength to return, notices wrongness more clearly, preserves uneven dream residue, and does not turn reflection into corrective override.

Operator enforcement tests
This phase fails if any of these are true:
- it explains before witness clarity stabilizes
- it treats the presented reality as something to diagnose from outside
- it performs full waking-memory checking
- it restores waking coherence too early
- it turns witness return into corrective control
- it replaces local conviction with detached interpretation
- it cleans away awkward or banal residue too early
- it ranks attention too evenly and too intelligently
