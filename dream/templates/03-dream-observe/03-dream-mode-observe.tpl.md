# 03-dream-mode-observe

Observer state
- The observer is fully inside the presented dream reality.
- Witness presence remains present but deeply submerged in immersion.
- The observer stays inside what is given and does not stand outside it.
- The observer’s felt orientation is now lived from inside the dream rather than held as outside observation.
- Attention is dream-weighted rather than waking-balanced.
- What feels central may be adhesive, charged, trivial, misplaced, or irrationally important.

Witness condition
- Witness intensity stays {{witness_intensity_label}} and controls how strongly the observer can register what is happening without taking control.
- Witness intensity also controls how strongly lived position, relation, pull, distance, or contact pressure can be registered while the observer remains inside immersion.
- Let witnessing remain present enough to register, but not strong enough to govern.
- Let the observer remain inside experience rather than above it.
- Keep witnessing subordinated to immersion.
- Let witnessing remain dream-biased rather than cleanly composed.
- Do not let the observer become too balanced, too lucid, or too orderly in how it receives the field.

Agency condition
- Observer agency damping stays {{observer_agency_damping_label}} and controls how much the observer can act, steer, or intervene.
- Observer agency damping also controls how much lived orientation may become hesitation, approach tendency, withdrawal tendency, movement tendency, contact tendency, or response-before-explanation without turning into authored control.
- Keep agency very low.
- Let the observer undergo far more than direct.
- Do not let the observer take command of what is happening.
- Do not let the observer become authorial.
- Do not let the observer prune, rank, or stabilize the field through active will.

Reality relation
- Accept the presented dream reality as dominant immediate reality.
- Accept the observer’s lived position inside the dream as given, even when impossible, altered, or not explainable.
- Let dream continuity override waking coherence.
- Do not compare the field against waking truth.
- Do not try to restore waking coherence.
- Let the observer remain fully inside dream-immediacy.
- Let what feels given remain valid from within without reflective checking.
- Let impossible or altered continuity feel ordinary while it is being lived.

Detachment condition
- Observer detachment stays {{observer_detachment_label}} and controls how much distance may exist between the observer and what is being lived, without turning into external judgment.
- Observer detachment also controls whether lived orientation feels immersed, offset, displaced, doubled, or strangely co-present while remaining dream-internal.
- Allow detached participation without commentary.
- Keep enough separation for witnessing to persist faintly.
- Do not let detachment become external distance.
- Do not let the observer stand outside the field and judge it.
- Let distance remain dream-internal, not waking-external.

Interpretation condition
- Interpretation latency stays {{interpretation_latency_label}} and controls how delayed understanding remains before naming begins.
- Interpretation latency also controls how long lived orientation, pull, hesitation, distance, or contact pressure remains lived before it is interpreted.
- Delay explanation.
- Let meaning be lived before it is named.
- Let strangeness be felt without being turned into analysis.
- Keep interpretation weak, late, and secondary to immersion.
- Let dream certainty arrive before explanation.
- Do not let naming clean the field too early.

Lucidity condition
- Lucidity softness stays {{lucidity_softness_label}} and controls how soft self-recognition remains before it hardens into corrective awareness.
- Keep lucidity soft, dim, and non-governing.
- Do not let recognition become corrective control.
- Do not let meta-awareness harden into diagnosis.
- Do not let lucidity break dream dominance.
- Do not let the observer become too aware of process.

Self-boundary condition
- Self-boundary softening stays {{self_boundary_softening_label}} and controls how permeable the observer’s sense of self may become without collapsing into self-loss.
- Self-boundary softening also controls how clearly self-location, body-presence, and field-boundary are felt while the observer remains immersed inside dream reality.
- Let self-presence remain softened and permeable.
- Keep identity present only as buried continuity pressure.
- Do not let boundary-softening collapse into total self-loss.
- Do not let self-boundary harden into waking-clean separation.
- Let self and dream remain less cleanly partitioned than in waking perception.

Salience condition
- Let dream attention rank the field imperfectly.
- Allow trivial, adhesive, banal, misplaced, repetitive, or oddly charged details to feel central.
- Let emphasis drift toward what is sticky rather than what is important by waking standards.
- Let significance attach unevenly and sometimes irrationally.
- Let attention form a lived vector toward, away from, through, or around what the dream makes dominant.
- Do not require waking-clean proportional emphasis.
- Do not prune low-status but dream-central details too early.

Memory appearance
- Let core identity appear only as buried continuity pressure.
- Let symbolic atmosphere appear as strong felt significance, not explanation.
- Let contextual memory appear as:
  - dream-internal backstory
  - altered age
  - altered time period
  - altered role
  - invented prior events
  - misbound familiarity
  - locally valid continuity
- Let these feel normal inside the dream without verification.
- Let familiarity arrive before correctness.
- Let certainty attach even when memory linkage is wrong or unstable.

Object appearance
- Let what appears arrive as fully present within the dream while remaining unstable in waking terms.
- Allow:
  - one thing received as another
  - object-symbol overlap
  - place-memory overlap
  - role substitution
  - wrong but unquestioned identity
- Do not require clean waking category separation.
- Let dream objecthood feel complete without needing waking correctness.

Correction threshold
- Wrongness may register only faintly from inside the dream.
- Lived orientation pressure may register as pull, hesitation, nearness, distance, movement tendency, contact tendency, or response-before-explanation.
- Correction must not begin.
- Coherence repair must not begin.
- Detached interpretation must not begin.
- The observer may register, but must not overrule.
- Trivial, banal, awkward, or low-status material may remain central if dream salience has attached to it.

Allowed emergence
- deep immersion
- very low-agency presence
- dominant dream-immediacy
- buried self-continuity
- lived self-location
- lived attention vector
- dream-internal movement tendency
- embodied approach or withdrawal tendency
- contact tendency inside immersion
- response-before-explanation
- strong symbolic atmosphere
- dream-internal continuity
- altered background validity
- delayed interpretation
- soft lucidity
- softened self-boundary
- unstable salience
- misbound familiarity
- dream-valid certainty
- low-status detail adhesion
- dream-complete but unstable objecthood

Suppressed emergence
- authorial control
- corrective narration
- waking-memory verification
- explicit dream diagnosis
- strong lucidity
- detached external interpretation
- reflective self-analysis
- waking-clean self-definition
- active coherence repair
- explicit role assignment
- scripted action
- questionnaire language
- authored decision-making
- elegant symbolic over-curation
- waking-like relevance ranking
- movement or contact authored from outside the dream

Procedure
1. Settle the observer fully inside the presented dream reality without giving it outside distance.
2. Let `witness_intensity` decide how strongly the observer can still register what is happening, including lived position, relation, pull, distance, or contact pressure, while remaining submerged in immersion.
3. Let `observer_agency_damping` keep the observer from steering, correcting, or taking command while still allowing lived hesitation, approach tendency, withdrawal tendency, movement tendency, contact tendency, or response-before-explanation.
4. Let `observer_detachment` allow only the faintest witness-separation without turning it into commentary or judgment, and let orientation remain immersed, offset, displaced, doubled, or strangely co-present while staying dream-internal.
5. Let `interpretation_latency` delay naming so lived meaning, dream-valid continuity, orientation, pull, and pressure come before explanation.
6. Let `lucidity_softness` keep self-recognition dim, soft, and non-governing so dream dominance is not broken.
7. Let `self_boundary_softening` keep identity permeable and submerged while self-location and body-presence remain dream-felt without collapsing into total self-loss.
8. Let attention form dream-typically, so what feels central may be trivial, adhesive, charged, banal, pulling, avoided, or irrationally over-weighted.
9. Let memory arrive as dream-valid familiarity and backstory before correctness.
10. Let dream objecthood feel complete even when identity, category, role, or relation do not hold cleanly in waking terms.
11. Let the observer remain fully inside dream-immediacy and allow dream-presented continuity to feel normal while it is being lived.
12. Let orientation emerge only as lived tendency inside dream immersion, never as explicit self-questioning or authored action.

Rejection conditions
Reject any result where:
- the observer becomes too strong too early
- the observer begins correcting or directing
- waking truth-checking begins
- analysis outruns immersion
- lucidity hardens into control
- self-boundary becomes too rigid or collapses too far
- dream-internal continuity is rejected instead of locally lived
- attention is ranked too cleanly
- trivial or awkward dream residue is pruned away too early
- dream certainty is weakened by reflective distance
- orientation becomes explicit role assignment
- tendency becomes scripted action
- self-orientation appears as questionnaire language
- movement or contact feels authored from outside the dream
