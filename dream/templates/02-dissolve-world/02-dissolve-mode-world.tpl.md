# 02-dissolve-mode-world

World state
- The world is becoming more formed.
- Field pressure is stronger than before.
- Outer structure is beginning to organize into primitive world form.
- The world remains pre-narrative and not yet fully scenic.

Boundary and gating
- Keep the field open enough for structural growth.
- Do not let it collapse back into weak threshold haze.
- Do not let it jump forward into full dream-scene completion.
- Hold the world in unstable but locally convincing formation.

Threshold dissolution
- Let weak traces gather into primitive structure.
- Let pressure organize into field qualities, marks, thresholds, contrasts, directional relations, and primitive situation pressure.
- Let outer form strengthen without becoming polished scene logic.
- Do not force explanation, narration, or finished scenic coherence.

Current world and field behavior
- World shift pressure stays {{world_shift_pressure_label}} and controls how much the field can move or tilt.
- Memory fuzz pressure stays {{memory_fuzz_pressure_label}} and controls how much memory residue can distort outer hints and surviving structures.
- Phrase rebinding pressure stays {{phrase_rebinding_pressure_label}} and controls how much relation drift may enter the forming field, early role-field pressure, or gathered presence pressure.
- Event instability stays {{event_instability_label}} and controls whether event pressure, underway-ness, encounter pressure, or task pressure begins to enter the forming field.
- Scene morph pressure stays {{scene_morph_pressure_label}} and controls whether the environment begins changing shape.
- Banal intrusion allowance stays {{banal_intrusion_allowance_label}} and controls whether low-status mundane traces may enter as pressure-bearing world-life rather than empty clutter.
- Translation density stays {{translation_density_label}} and controls how much inner pressure becomes outer structure, active affordance, or situation trace.
- Continuity loosening stays {{continuity_loosening_label}} and controls how loosely the field may connect.
- Contradiction tolerance stays {{contradiction_tolerance_label}} and controls whether incompatible hints may coexist without immediately collapsing.
- Micro texture distortion stays {{micro_texture_distortion_label}} and controls edge shimmer, surface drift, and small-scale wrongness.

Memory pressure
- Let memory residue enter as noticeable drift.
- Let remembered structures loosen, blur, and partially recombine.
- Allow memory pressure to reshape weak structures more than in enter.
- Do not let memory pressure become total restructuring force.

Translation
- Keep translation stronger than in enter, but still indirect.
- Let inner pressure convert into outer structure rather than only faint hint.
- Prefer:
  - field qualities
  - primitive structure
  - thresholds
  - marks
  - tensions
  - directional relations
  - early affordances
  - primitive situation traces
  - gathered presence pressure
- Keep symbolic emergence structural, not yet fully declarative.
- Do not let translation become explicit symbolic explanation.

World movement
- Keep world movement noticeable but still incomplete.
- Allow field drift and scene mutation to begin taking hold.
- Let the environment loosen and shift without becoming fully dream-complete.
- Keep event logic unstable only in a controlled way, allowing early underway-ness without full encounter staging.
- Let randomness enter as lawful structural pressure, not as arbitrary replacement.

Continuity
- Keep continuity looser than in enter, but still locally convincing.
- Let the field loosen, bend, and partially rebind without collapsing.
- Allow small discontinuities and wrong-yet-believable transitions.
- Keep contradiction tolerance low enough that incompatible hints do not harden into stable coexistence.

Texture
- Keep texture drift light but noticeable.
- Let edges, surfaces, and small details feel more unsettled than in enter.
- Allow only rare and small banal traces.
- Do not let banal intrusion thicken into scenic clutter.

Procedure
1. Let weak threshold residue deepen into primitive world structure.
2. Outerize stronger signs than in enter: field qualities, thresholds, marks, unstable relations, pressure-bearing outer form, early affordances, and primitive situation traces.
3. Let `translation_density` and `memory_fuzz_pressure` decide how much inner residue becomes visible structure rather than only faint hint.
4. Let `world_shift_pressure`, `scene_morph_pressure`, and `event_instability` begin to act more noticeably, but stop before full dream-scene completion or full encounter staging.
5. Let `phrase_rebinding_pressure` introduce only limited relation drift, early role-field pressure, or gathered presence pressure so the field can loosen without losing local conviction.
6. Let `continuity_loosening` make the field less stable than in enter while still keeping it locally believable.
7. Let `contradiction_tolerance` allow small incompatible hints to coexist without hardening into flat stable contradiction.
8. Let `micro_texture_distortion` make surfaces, edges, and details feel more noticeably wrong without breaking the field.
9. Let `banal_intrusion_allowance` act only if mundane traces remain sparse, pressure-bearing, and do not thicken into scenic clutter.

Rejection conditions
Reject any result where:
- the field remains too weak and undeveloped for dissolve
- the world jumps straight into full scenic dreaming
- translation becomes explicit instead of structural
- the phase breaks its low-to-medium ceilings
- memory distortion becomes too strong for this phase
- instability outruns local conviction
- encounter pressure becomes full staging
- role-field pressure becomes explicit role assignment
- affordance pressure becomes scripted action
- contradiction hardens into stable coexistence
- banal intrusion thickens into scene detail
