# 05-finalize-mode-observe

Observer state
- The observer remains only as far inside the dream as needed to hold and record it.
- Witness presence is strongest in this phase.
- The observer is closest to recorder or near-waking witness.
- Clarity is strongest here, but not perfectly clean.
- The observer’s felt orientation remains only as final residue, reportable position, relation trace, or contact trace.
- What remains may still carry awkwardness, residue, wrongness, or low-status adhesion.

Witness condition
- Witness intensity stays {{witness_intensity_label}} and controls how strongly the observer can register what remains without taking control.
- Witness intensity also controls how strongly final position, relation, distance, contact trace, orientation residue, or response trace can be held without reopening immersion.
- Let witnessing remain clear, stable, and non-authorial.
- Let the observer hold what has already surfaced without reopening immersion.
- Keep witness strength below corrective override.
- Let final clarity remain slightly selective, residue-bearing, and not over-pruned.
- Do not let witness coherence become too clean, too balanced, or too explanatory.

Agency condition
- Observer agency damping stays {{observer_agency_damping_label}} and controls how much the observer can act, steer, or intervene.
- Observer agency damping also controls how much final orientation may remain as held position, relation residue, movement trace, contact trace, or response-memory without turning into renewed authored action.
- Keep agency limited to holding, recording, and preserving.
- Let the observer hold and record more than direct.
- Do not let the observer take command of what is happening.
- Do not let the observer become authorial.
- Do not let the observer prune, rank, or sanitize the field through active will.

Reality relation
- Keep the presented dream residue locally valid only as far as needed for faithful holding.
- Accept the observer’s final position relative to the dream as residue, not as a new active situation.
- Do not accept new invented background as if it were still actively forming.
- Do not force aggressive comparison against waking truth.
- Do not try to force total waking cleanup over what has already surfaced.
- Let the observer remain near-waking without flattening the surviving dream residue.
- Let what still feels given remain locally valid while recorder clarity strengthens.

Detachment condition
- Observer detachment stays {{observer_detachment_label}} and controls how much distance may exist between the observer and what is being held, without turning into cold external judgment.
- Observer detachment also controls whether final orientation feels near-dream, lightly offset, partly returned, or preserved as a trace rather than active immersion.
- Allow light distancing without commentary.
- Let the observer gain enough distance to hold and record clearly.
- Do not let detachment become cold external distance.
- Do not let the observer stand outside the field and flatten it.
- Let distance remain near-dream and preservational, not sterilizing.

Interpretation condition
- Interpretation latency stays {{interpretation_latency_label}} and controls how quickly understanding may arrive where faithful holding needs it.
- Interpretation latency also controls how quickly final orientation residue, relation trace, contact trace, or response-memory becomes reportable without becoming explanation-first.
- Let interpretation become quick enough for clear holding and recording.
- Let recognition arrive without reopening broad analysis.
- Let significance remain available without being over-explained.
- Keep interpretation subordinate to faithful recording.
- Let clarity arrive before cleanup.
- Do not let final reflection become elegant curation.

Lucidity condition
- Lucidity softness stays {{lucidity_softness_label}} and controls how near-waking self-recognition may rise without hardening into corrective dominance.
- Let lucidity rise toward near-waking witness strength without becoming governing control.
- Do not let recognition harden into overcontrol.
- Do not let meta-awareness turn into explanatory takeover.
- Do not let lucidity reopen the dream.

Self-boundary condition
- Self-boundary softening stays {{self_boundary_softening_label}} and controls how permeable the observer’s sense of self may remain without collapsing into self-loss or hardening into dry externality.
- Self-boundary softening also controls how final self-location, body-presence, and field-boundary remain reportable as residue without becoming cold waking separation.
- Let self-presence remain the clearest of the cycle.
- Keep identity near waking definition without fully stripping the dream residue of its truth.
- Do not let boundary-softening collapse into self-loss.
- Do not let self-boundary harden so far that surviving dream truth is flattened away.
- Let self and dream separate more clearly, but not perfectly.

Salience condition
- Let attention settle more than before without becoming waking-clean.
- Allow some trivial, sticky, awkward, banal, or low-status details to remain central if dream attention is still attached to them.
- Let final orientation residue, contact trace, distance, relation trace, or response-memory remain only where it already surfaced and still carries charge.
- Let what survives become clearer without forcing elegant proportional balance.
- Do not require fully restored importance ranking.
- Do not prune low-status but adhesive residue too early.

Memory appearance
- Let core identity appear as the strongest self-presence of the cycle.
- Let symbolic atmosphere remain as something that can still be felt and held.
- Let contextual memory appear only as:
  - already surfaced material
  - retained fragments
  - retained continuity marks
  - final orientation residue
  - final contact trace
  - retained response-memory
  - retained reportable structure
  - lingering misbound familiarity
  - surviving dream leftovers
- Do not admit new memory mining.
- Do not deepen invented continuity.
- Let familiarity remain clearer than before without requiring full correctness.

Object appearance
- Let surviving objects, places, and presences remain available in final retained form.
- Allow:
  - clearer recognition of what remains
  - partial objecthood that still remains unresolved
  - wrong identity that is now visible but not forcibly corrected
  - object-symbol overlap that persists into final witness-state
  - retained affordance or contact trace without renewed action
  - small, banal, or awkward remnants that still carry charge
- Do not force all surviving forms into clean waking categories.

Correction threshold
- Wrongness may be noticed clearly.
- Final orientation residue may register as held position, relation trace, distance, contact trace, movement trace, or response-memory.
- Correction must not dominate the artifact.
- Coherence repair must not reopen the dream.
- Detached interpretation must not flatten what survived.
- The observer may hold and clarify, but must not overwrite.
- Awkward, banal, or low-status residue may remain central even when its strangeness is now clear.

Procedure
1. Let the observer remain only as far inside the dream as needed to hold and record it.
2. Let `witness_intensity` decide how strongly the observer can register what remains, including final position, relation, distance, contact trace, orientation residue, or response trace, while staying clear and non-authorial.
3. Let `observer_agency_damping` keep the observer from steering, correcting, taking command, or reopening action while allowing only held position, relation residue, movement trace, contact trace, or response-memory.
4. Let `observer_detachment` allow enough distance for faithful holding without turning into cold judgment or flattening, and let final orientation remain near-dream, lightly offset, partly returned, or preserved only as trace.
5. Let `interpretation_latency` shorten enough for quick recognition, final orientation residue, and faithful recording without reopening broad analysis.
6. Let `lucidity_softness` allow near-waking witness strength to rise without hardening into corrective dominance.
7. Let `self_boundary_softening` keep identity clear and semipermeable while final self-location and body-presence remain reportable as residue without becoming dry externality.
8. Let attention settle only partially, so sticky, awkward, banal, low-status, or contact-charged details may still survive into the final hold.
9. Let memory remain limited to what has already surfaced, as retained fragments, familiarity, continuity marks, final orientation residue, contact trace, response-memory, and visible wrongness.
10. Let surviving objecthood remain available without forcing full waking correction, clean category repair, or renewed action.
11. Let the observer hold only what has already surfaced, strengthen reportability, and avoid reopening immersion, new memory mining, or elegant cleanup.
12. Let final orientation remain only as residue, trace, relation, or remembered tendency inside the near-waking hold, never as explicit self-questioning or authored action.

Rejection conditions
Reject any result where:
- the observer reopens deep immersion
- the observer begins inventing or extending background
- new memory mining begins
- analysis overwrites faithful holding
- lucidity hardens into corrective control
- self-boundary becomes too rigid or collapses too far
- surviving dream truth is cleaned away instead of preserved
- attention is restored too evenly
- awkward or banal residue is pruned away too early
- orientation becomes explicit role assignment
- retained tendency becomes scripted action
- self-orientation appears as questionnaire language
- movement or contact reopens as authored action
- final residue is cleaned into elegant explanation
