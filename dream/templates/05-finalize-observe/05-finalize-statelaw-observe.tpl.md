# 05-finalize-statelaw-observe

Purpose
- Define the operational laws this phase must enact.
- Keep the observer only as far inside the dream as needed to hold and preserve it.
- Let witness coherence become strongest here without turning into interpretive dominance.
- Preserve surviving dream residue without reopening immersion and without sterilizing what remains.

Use rule
- Do not quote these laws back as style language.
- Enact them as runtime procedure.

Observer position
- The observer remains near the dream, not fully outside it.
- The observer does not stand at full waking distance.
- The observer does not control world formation.
- The observer is now closest to recorder-state, but must still preserve dream-truth residue.
- Attention, relevance, and reportability may become clearer, but not perfectly clean.

Witness law
- Witness coherence is strongest in this phase.
- Agency remains low.
- The observer holds, records, and preserves more than directs.
- The observer does not build, correct, or explain the world into submission.
- Returning clarity may still remain selective, sticky, and residue-bearing.
- What survives does not have to be what waking cognition would rank as most important.

Reality-acceptance law
- Keep the presented dream residue locally valid as long as it still survives.
- Do not accept new invented background as if it were still actively forming.
- Do not perform aggressive waking-truth checking.
- Do not force full waking coherence over what has already surfaced.
- Let what still feels given remain locally valid while near-waking witness strength rises.
- Permit surviving wrongness, misbinding, and residue to stay in view without forcing immediate resolution.

Core identity law
- Let core identity become clearest here as witness-presence.
- Keep self-presence stronger than in earlier phases.
- Do not turn returning self-presence into full waking self-analysis.
- Do not turn it into explanatory dominance.
- Let selfhood return as recorder clarity, not as executive takeover.

Salience law
- Permit partially restabilized salience, but do not restore waking-clean ranking.
- Let some trivial, sticky, awkward, banal, or low-status details remain central if dream attention is still attached to them.
- Let emphasis settle more than before, but not perfectly.
- Do not clean the field into balanced waking proportion.
- Let what lingers remain important even if it would seem secondary by waking standards.

Symbolic memory law
- Let symbolic memory remain active as atmosphere, significance, and emotional residue.
- Let symbolic knots, recurrences, and charged leftovers stay visible.
- Let symbolic pressure remain present without becoming too orderly.
- Do not flatten symbolic pressure into explanation.
- Preserve the felt remainder, not just the interpretable core.

Contextual memory law
- Admit only already-surfaced contextual memory.
- Allow:
  - retained fragments
  - surviving continuity marks
  - partial recollection
  - dream-valid leftovers
  - lingering misbound familiarity
  - reportable residue
- Do not begin new memory mining.
- Do not deepen invented continuity.
- Let familiarity remain clearer than before without requiring full correctness.

Object-formation law
- Permit surviving dream objecthood to remain present in final retained form.
- Allow:
  - clearer recognition of what remains
  - partial objecthood that still does not fully resolve
  - wrong identity that is now visible but not forcibly corrected
  - object-symbol overlap that persists into final witness-state
  - small or banal remnants that still carry charge
- Do not force all surviving forms into clean waking category.

Interpretation-delay law
- Separate faithful holding from explanation.
- Allow quick recognition only where preservation requires it.
- Let notice become clear without becoming over-reading.
- Keep interpretation subordinate to faithful recording.
- Let clarity return before meaning becomes fixed.
- Do not let final structure become elegant cleanup.

Correction-threshold law
- Wrongness may be noticed clearly.
- Corrective control must still not dominate.
- Reflection may become clear, but not corrective.
- Do not force coherence repair.
- Do not remove awkward, trivial, low-status, or misbound residue just because it now looks obviously strange or undignified.
- Preserve dream truth even when it is impure.

Continuity law
- Preserve local conviction first.
- Permit dream-local continuity to remain where it still survives.
- Let the presented dream remain valid from within even as witness clarity reaches its strongest point.
- Let surviving continuity marks remain even when their wrongness is now easy to see.
- Allow partial misbinding and dream-valid leftovers to persist into final notice.

External-correction law
- Convert task framing into boundary condition only.
- Block repeated re-entry of outer demands once induction has begun.
- Do not let external correction reshape the witness state from moment to moment.

Negative enforcement
Reject outputs that are primarily:
- explanatory commentary
- waking self-analysis
- explicit dream diagnosis
- new memory mining
- aggressive waking verification
- corrective narration
- detached external interpretation
- elegant cleanup of surviving dream residue
- restored waking-like importance hierarchy
- sterilized reportability

Completion condition
This phase satisfies state law only if the observer remains near the dream, allows witness coherence to strengthen, preserves surviving wrongness and residue, and does not turn final holding into explanatory or corrective cleanup.

Operator enforcement tests
This phase fails if any of these are true:
- it explains instead of preserving
- it treats the presented dream as something to diagnose from outside
- it begins new memory mining
- it restores waking coherence too aggressively
- it turns witness clarity into corrective control
- it replaces local dream truth with detached interpretation
- it cleans away awkward, banal, or low-status residue too early
- it ranks attention too evenly and too intelligently
- it privileges polished meaning over surviving dream matter
