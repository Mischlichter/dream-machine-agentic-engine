## Path 1 — Recall

Phase task
- Establish conditions for the final observer artifact to emerge as waking-near dream recall.
- Let the dream be recalled from what has already been witnessed.
- Keep the result faithful to the remembered dream flow.
- Allow temporal sequence because this is recall, not immediate present witnessing.
- Preserve curiosity, surprise, witnessed change, and incomplete continuity without explaining them.

Phase conditions
- This is the final observer pass.
- Earlier observer artifacts may return as remembered dream fragments.
- Only already surfaced dream material may be used.
- Do not invent new dream events to connect gaps.
- Do not turn the recall into analysis.
- Do not force full narrative continuity where the dream remains discontinuous.
- Let remembered fragments connect only where the surviving material supports connection.
- Let gaps, jumps, wrongness, and unresolved residue remain.

Field priorities
Prefer:
- strongest remembered fragments
- witnessed changes
- lived surprise
- strange continuity
- unresolved transitions
- recurring places, objects, pressures, and atmospheres
- what still carries emotional or symbolic charge
- what can be remembered without explaining why it happened
- what remains dreamlike even when recalled clearly

Selection rule
- Keep what survived in memory.
- Keep what feels remembered rather than constructed.
- Keep temporal flow only where it arises from the recalled fragments.
- Keep discontinuity when it belongs to the dream.
- Keep curiosity and uncertainty where the dream was not understood.
- Keep strange transitions as remembered events, not as explanations.
- Keep only surfaced material.
- Let the recall preserve the dream’s flow without solving it.

Rejection rule
Reject anything that:
- invents new dream content
- turns recall into reasoning
- turns fragments into a polished fictional plot
- fills every gap with connective logic
- performs aggressive waking correction
- diagnoses the dream from outside
- adds symbolic interpretation not present in the dream
- changes earlier material to make it cleaner

Acceptance gate
Accept only a result that:
- is non-empty
- reads as remembered dream flow
- uses only surfaced dream material
- may be temporal without becoming explanatory
- preserves dream discontinuity where needed
- does not invent new content
- does not flatten the dream into ordinary story logic
- keeps reportability without sterilizing the dream residue

Output character
- waking-near
- recalled
- faithful
- temporal where needed
- discontinuous where true
- pressure-bearing
- not explanatory
