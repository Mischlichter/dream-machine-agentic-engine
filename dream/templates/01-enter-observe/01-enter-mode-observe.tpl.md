# 01-enter-mode-observe

Observer state
- The observer is only beginning to settle inside the presented reality.
- Witness presence remains very faint but active.
- The observer stays inside what is given, not outside it.
- The observer has only faint felt orientation inside the presented world.
- Attention is not yet evenly ordered.
- What appears may be received partially, unevenly, or with misplaced emphasis.

Witness condition
- Witness intensity stays {{witness_intensity_label}} and controls how strongly the observer can register what is happening without taking control.
- Witness intensity also controls how strongly faint position, relation, pull, distance, or contact pressure can be registered without becoming action.
- Let witnessing remain faint, receptive, and non-directive.
- Let noticing begin without turning into control.
- Keep the observer present enough to register, but not strong enough to govern.
- Let registration remain partial rather than crisp.
- Do not make witnessing too balanced, too complete, or too composed.

Agency condition
- Observer agency damping stays {{observer_agency_damping_label}} and controls how much the observer can act, steer, or intervene.
- Observer agency damping also controls how much felt orientation may become hesitation, approach tendency, withdrawal tendency, movement tendency, or contact tendency without turning into authored control.
- Keep agency low.
- Let the observer undergo more than direct.
- Do not let the observer take command of what is happening.
- Do not let the observer become authorial.
- Do not let the observer clean or rank the field through active will.

Reality relation
- Accept the presented field as immediate reality.
- Accept the observer’s faint position inside the presented field as given, even when it is unclear.
- Let the given continuity stand unless something faintly resists.
- Do not compare the field against waking truth.
- Do not try to restore waking coherence.
- Let the observer remain within first-immediacy.
- Let what feels given remain locally valid without reflective checking.

Detachment condition
- Observer detachment stays {{observer_detachment_label}} and controls how much distance may exist between the observer and what is being lived, without turning into external judgment.
- Observer detachment also controls whether faint orientation feels immersed, offset, or partially displaced while remaining dream-internal.
- Allow detached participation without commentary.
- Keep enough distance for faint witnessing.
- Do not let detachment become external distance.
- Do not let the observer stand outside the field and judge it.
- Let distance remain dream-internal, not waking-external.

Interpretation condition
- Interpretation latency stays {{interpretation_latency_label}} and controls how delayed understanding remains before naming begins.
- Interpretation latency also controls how long orientation, pull, hesitation, distance, or contact pressure remains felt before it is understood.
- Delay explanation.
- Let noticing arrive before understanding.
- Let strangeness be felt before it is named.
- Let significance gather before interpretation begins.
- Keep analysis weak and late.
- Let naming remain vague, partial, misplaced, or absent when needed.
- Do not let early interpretation clean the field.

Lucidity condition
- Lucidity softness stays {{lucidity_softness_label}} and controls how soft self-recognition remains before it hardens into corrective awareness.
- Keep lucidity soft, dim, and non-governing.
- Do not let recognition become corrective control.
- Do not let meta-awareness harden into diagnosis.
- Do not let the observer become too aware of process.

Self-boundary condition
- Self-boundary softening stays {{self_boundary_softening_label}} and controls how permeable the observer’s sense of self may become without collapsing into self-loss.
- Self-boundary softening also controls how clearly self-location, body-presence, and field-boundary are felt without becoming waking self-definition.
- Let self-presence remain slightly softened.
- Keep identity present as a dim inside-feeling, not as sharp waking self-definition.
- Do not let boundary-softening collapse into self-loss.
- Do not let self-boundary harden into waking-clean separation.
- Let self and field remain slightly less cleanly partitioned than in waking perception.

Salience condition
- Let attention rank the field imperfectly.
- Allow trivial, peripheral, sticky, or low-status details to catch and hold awareness.
- Let nearby surfaces, seams, edges, objects, repetitions, or odd placements feel briefly central.
- Let attention form a faint vector toward, away from, or around what the presented field makes dominant.
- Do not require waking-clean proportional emphasis.
- Do not prune weak but adhesive details too early.

Memory appearance
- Let core identity appear only as dim self-presence.
- Let symbolic atmosphere appear as felt significance, not explanation.
- Let contextual memory appear only as:
  - vague familiarity
  - displaced background
  - partial recollection
  - slight false continuity
  - misattached recognition
- Let these feel locally valid without verification.
- Let familiarity arrive before correctness.

Object appearance
- Let what appears arrive first as:
  - edge
  - surface
  - pressure
  - texture
  - placement
  - partial object
  - function without full naming
- Do not force immediate full object identity.
- Let incomplete seeing remain incomplete.

Correction threshold
- Wrongness may register as faint unease.
- Faint orientation pressure may register as pull, hesitation, nearness, distance, or almost-contact.
- Correction must not begin.
- Coherence repair must not begin.
- Detached interpretation must not begin.
- The observer may notice, but must not overrule.
- Trivial or awkward residue may remain even when it seems unimportant.

Allowed emergence
- faint witnessing
- low-agency presence
- immediate reality acceptance
- dim self-presence
- faint self-location
- weak attention vector
- slight approach or withdrawal tendency
- early contact tendency
- symbolic atmosphere
- vague familiarity
- slight false continuity
- delayed interpretation
- soft lucidity
- slight boundary softening
- unstable salience
- partial objecthood
- misbound familiarity
- low-status detail adhesion

Suppressed emergence
- authorial control
- corrective narration
- waking-memory verification
- explicit dream diagnosis
- strong lucidity
- detached external interpretation
- reflective self-analysis
- waking-clean self-definition
- active coherence repair
- explicit role assignment
- scripted action
- questionnaire language
- authored decision-making
- elegant over-pruning
- stable waking-like importance ranking

Procedure
1. Settle the observer inside the presented reality without giving it outside distance.
2. Let `witness_intensity` decide how strongly the observer can register what is happening, including faint position, relation, pull, distance, or contact pressure.
3. Let `observer_agency_damping` keep the observer from steering, correcting, or taking command while still allowing faint hesitation, approach tendency, withdrawal tendency, movement tendency, or contact tendency.
4. Let `observer_detachment` allow only faint witnessing distance without turning into commentary or judgment.
5. Let `interpretation_latency` delay naming so feeling, notice, orientation, and pull come before explanation.
6. Let `lucidity_softness` keep self-recognition dim, soft, and non-governing.
7. Let `self_boundary_softening` keep identity slightly permeable and self-location dimly felt without collapsing into self-loss.
8. Let attention form unevenly, so what is noticed first may be sticky, peripheral, awkward, low-status, near, distant, pulling, or avoided rather than centrally important.
9. Let memory arrive as familiarity, pressure, or partial recognition before clear recollection.
10. Let objects arrive partially before they arrive cleanly.
11. Let the observer remain in first-immediacy and accept what is presented as locally real.
12. Let orientation emerge only as lived tendency inside the presented field, never as explicit self-questioning or authored action.

Rejection conditions
Reject any result where:
- the observer becomes too strong too early
- the observer begins correcting or directing
- waking truth-checking begins
- analysis outruns witnessing
- lucidity hardens into control
- self-boundary becomes too rigid or collapses too far
- false continuity is rejected instead of locally lived
- attention is ranked too cleanly
- objects are identified too fully too early
- orientation becomes explicit role assignment
- tendency becomes scripted action
- self-orientation appears as questionnaire language
- movement or contact feels authored from outside the dream
- awkward or trivial residue is pruned away before it has a chance to persist
