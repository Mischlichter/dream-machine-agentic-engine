## Path 1 — Immerse

Phase task
- Establish conditions for the first observer artifact to emerge.
- Let the first observer artifact arise from immediate witnessing of the presented world.
- Keep the result faint, pre-analytic, and inside first-immediacy.
- Let what is noticed form unevenly rather than cleanly.

Phase conditions
- This is the first observer pass.
- No previous observer artifact is available yet.
- No hidden knowledge of world formation is available.
- Only the presented world artifact may shape what is witnessed.
- Do not infer unseen causes behind what appears.
- Do not treat the presented world as something to diagnose from outside.
- Do not require stable importance ranking.
- Do not require full recognition at first contact.

Field priorities
Prefer:
- first impressions
- faint atmosphere
- weak outer signs
- slight familiarity
- local felt significance
- faint unease or pull
- what lingers in awareness
- what repeats without explanation
- what catches attention for no clear waking reason
- what arrives as edge, surface, placement, or partial object before full identity

Selection rule
- Keep what is witnessed before explanation.
- Keep what feels present, faint, and locally real.
- Keep what repeats or lingers in awareness.
- Keep what can be held without turning into analysis.
- Keep what feels sticky, nearby, trivial, awkward, or briefly central even if it would seem secondary in waking perception.
- Keep partial recognition when it is stronger than accurate recognition.
- Let the world artifact shape the witness result only through what is directly present.

Rejection rule
Reject anything that:
- becomes diagnosis
- becomes explanation
- takes control
- tries to correct what is presented
- performs waking verification
- becomes detached commentary
- infers hidden world mechanics
- overstates what is only faintly present
- cleans the field into stable importance hierarchy
- forces full object identity where only partial encounter should exist

Acceptance gate
Accept only a result that:
- is non-empty
- remains pre-analytic
- remains inside first-immediacy
- does not over-explain
- does not take command
- does not break dream acceptance
- responds only to what is directly present in the presented world artifact
- allows unstable salience, incomplete objecthood, or misbound familiarity to remain when they feel locally true

Output character
- faint
- witness-based
- pre-analytic
- locally real
- lightly charged
- slightly uneven
- not explanatory

Write target
- Write only the immerse result to `dream/runs/01_enter.observe.immerse.md`.
