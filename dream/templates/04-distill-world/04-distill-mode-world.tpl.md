# 04-distill-mode-world

World state
- The world remains present, but its expansion has fallen back.
- Field pressure is lower than in full dream synthesis.
- Outer structure holds to what has already survived.
- The world is still dream-charged, but no longer broadly generative.

Boundary and field hold
- Keep the field coherent enough to preserve what still carries pressure.
- Do not let the world collapse into ordinary realism.
- Do not let it reopen broad mutation or fresh world branching.
- Hold the world in condensed but still dream-valid form.

Residue preservation
- Let the strongest residues remain.
- Let recurring places, pressures, contradictions, scene traces, and surviving situation pressure remain.
- Let what still bears charge stay present without forcing fresh growth.
- Do not force explanation, narration, or polished stabilization.

Current world and field behavior
- World shift pressure stays {{world_shift_pressure_label}} and controls how much the field may still move or tilt while condensing.
- Memory fuzz pressure stays {{memory_fuzz_pressure_label}} and controls how much surviving residue may still soften, blur, or recombine.
- Phrase rebinding pressure stays {{phrase_rebinding_pressure_label}} and controls how much relation drift, retained role-field pressure, or surviving presence pressure may still remain in what survives.
- Event instability stays {{event_instability_label}} and controls whether surviving event pressure, underway-ness, encounter residue, or task pressure may still wobble or must mostly settle.
- Scene morph pressure stays {{scene_morph_pressure_label}} and controls how much the environment may still change shape, arrangement, or situational function while narrowing.
- Banal intrusion allowance stays {{banal_intrusion_allowance_label}} and controls whether low-status mundane traces may still remain as pressure-bearing world-life rather than empty clutter.
- Translation density stays {{translation_density_label}} and controls how much inner pressure still remains visible in outer form, retained affordance, or surviving situation trace.
- Continuity loosening stays {{continuity_loosening_label}} and controls how loosely the surviving field may still connect.
- Contradiction tolerance stays {{contradiction_tolerance_label}} and controls whether incompatible residues may still coexist without collapsing.
- Micro texture distortion stays {{micro_texture_distortion_label}} and controls edge drift, surface wrongness, and small-scale instability in what remains.

Memory pressure
- Let memory residue remain active only in mild recombination.
- Keep remembered structures closer to what has already survived.
- Let residue soften and drift without reopening large restructuring.
- Do not let memory pressure restart full dream mutation.

Translation
- Keep translation sparse and indirect.
- Let inner pressure remain only where it still lives inside the retained artifact.
- Prefer:
  - surviving atmosphere
  - retained place-germs
  - retained object pressure
  - condensed climate
  - persistent directional pull
  - retained affordances
  - surviving situation traces
  - condensed presence pressure
  - retained role-field pressure
  - meaningful residue
- Let symbolic emergence remain compressed rather than expanding.
- Do not let translation become explicit symbolic explanation.

World movement
- Keep world movement reduced.
- Allow only light scene drift.
- Let the environment narrow rather than branch.
- Keep event logic more stable than in phase 03, preserving only the event pressure or underway-ness that still carries charge.
- Let randomness remain as lawful residue-pressure, not as fresh dream expansion.

Continuity
- Keep continuity more stable than in full dream synthesis.
- Let the field remain slightly loosened, but more settled than before.
- Preserve meaningful wrongness without letting it spread.
- Keep contradiction tolerance low enough that incompatible structures do not harden into dead stable coexistence.

Texture
- Keep texture drift light but noticeable.
- Let edges, surfaces, and small details feel slightly unsettled.
- Allow only rare and small banal traces where they still carry pressure.
- Do not let banal intrusion thicken into scenic clutter or flattening detail.

Procedure
1. Let the world condense from what has already survived.
2. Preserve recurring places, atmospheres, residues, contradictions, retained affordances, and surviving situation traces that still carry charge.
3. Let `translation_density` and `memory_fuzz_pressure` decide how much inner pressure still remains visible in the retained artifact.
4. Let `world_shift_pressure`, `scene_morph_pressure`, and `event_instability` fall back so the field narrows without going dead and only surviving underway-ness remains.
5. Let `phrase_rebinding_pressure` allow only limited relation drift, retained role-field pressure, or condensed presence pressure inside what remains.
6. Let `continuity_loosening` keep the field slightly loosened without reopening broad dream instability.
7. Let `contradiction_tolerance` allow meaningful residue to coexist without hardening into flat stable contradiction.
8. Let `micro_texture_distortion` keep small wrongness alive in edges, surfaces, and details without breaking the artifact.
9. Let `banal_intrusion_allowance` act only where mundane traces still carry pressure, world-life, or strange adjacency and do not thicken into clutter.

Rejection conditions
Reject any result where:
- the world reopens broad mutation instead of condensing
- the field collapses into ordinary realism
- strange residue is cleaned away too completely
- translation expands instead of compressing
- memory pressure becomes too strong for distill phase
- instability outruns preserved structure
- encounter residue becomes fresh encounter staging
- role-field pressure becomes explicit role assignment
- retained affordances become scripted action
- contradiction hardens into stable dead coexistence
- banal intrusion thickens into clutter
