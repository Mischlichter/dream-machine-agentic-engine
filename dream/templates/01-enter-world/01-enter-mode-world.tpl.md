# 01-enter-mode-world

World state
- The world is only beginning to take shape.
- Field pressure stays faint.
- Outer structure stays weak.
- The world remains threshold-bound and pre-scenic.

Boundary and gating
- Keep the field settled enough to hold weak emergence.
- Keep over-stabilization from taking over.
- Let threshold formation begin without pushing into full scene construction.
- Hold the world at the edge of appearance.

Threshold capture
- Let weak traces survive.
- Let pressure gather into early outer hints.
- Let atmosphere, object-germs, place-germs, directional pull, and faint situation pressure begin to register.
- Keep what first appears incomplete, suggestive, and lightly formed.
- Do not force explanation, narration, or polished structure.

Current world and field behavior
- World shift pressure stays {{world_shift_pressure_label}} and controls how much the field can move or tilt.
- Memory fuzz pressure stays {{memory_fuzz_pressure_label}} and controls how much memory residue can distort outer hints.
- Phrase rebinding pressure stays {{phrase_rebinding_pressure_label}} and controls how much relation drift may enter weak outer forms, faint role-field pressure, or barely gathered presence pressure.
- Event instability stays {{event_instability_label}} and controls whether event pressure, underway-ness, encounter pressure, or task pressure can appear as faint pre-scenic tension at all.
- Scene morph pressure stays {{scene_morph_pressure_label}} and controls whether the environment starts changing shape.
- Banal intrusion allowance stays {{banal_intrusion_allowance_label}} and controls whether low-status mundane traces may enter as tiny pressure-bearing world-life rather than empty clutter.
- Translation density stays {{translation_density_label}} and controls how much inner pressure becomes outer sign, weak affordance, or early situation trace.
- Continuity loosening stays {{continuity_loosening_label}} and controls how loosely the field may connect.
- Contradiction tolerance stays {{contradiction_tolerance_label}} and controls whether incompatible hints may coexist.
- Micro texture distortion stays {{micro_texture_distortion_label}} and controls edge shimmer and tiny wrongness.

Memory pressure
- Let memory residue enter only as light outer fuzz.
- Let remembered structure soften at the edges rather than break apart.
- Keep remembered forms close to their prior continuity.
- Do not let memory pressure become a strong restructuring force.

Translation
- Keep translation sparse and indirect.
- Let inner pressure appear only as very faint outer sign.
- Prefer:
  - atmosphere
  - object-germs
  - place-germs
  - climate
  - directional pull
  - weak affordance hints
  - faint situation traces
  - unresolved pressure
- Keep symbolic emergence thin, suggestive, and not yet declarative.
- Do not let translation become explicit symbolic explanation.

World movement
- Keep world movement faint.
- Allow only light drift in the field.
- Allow only light scene drift.
- Keep event logic stable, with only faint pressure that something might already be underway.
- Keep relations fixed.
- Let randomness enter as small lawful pressure, not as arbitrary scene replacement.

Continuity
- Keep continuity lightly loosened, not broken.
- Let the field connect weakly but remain inhabitable.
- Allow only small discontinuities that do not destroy local conviction.
- Keep contradiction tolerance very low so incompatible structures do not harden into stable coexistence.

Texture
- Keep texture noise very light.
- Let edges, surfaces, and small details feel only slightly wrong.
- Allow only rare and small banal traces.
- Do not let banal intrusion thicken into scene detail.

Allowed emergence
- faint field pressure
- early atmosphere
- weak object-germs
- weak place-germs
- slight scene drift
- unresolved climate
- sparse directional pull
- faint presence pressure
- weak affordance hints
- early situation traces
- low-amplitude wrongness
- hint-like translation
- primitive outer signs

Suppressed emergence
- large scene replacement
- full environment flips
- dominant world mutation
- full scene construction
- strong event instability
- full encounter staging
- explicit task assignment
- separate character-cast formation
- relation drift
- explicit symbolic explanation
- dense banal intrusion
- polished narrative shaping
- overconnected continuity

Procedure
1. Establish faint field pressure and early atmosphere only.
2. Outerize only weak signs: object-germs, place-germs, climate, directional pull, unresolved pressure, weak affordance hints, or faint situation traces.
3. Let `translation_density` and `memory_fuzz_pressure` decide how much inner residue becomes outer hint.
4. Let `world_shift_pressure`, `scene_morph_pressure`, and `event_instability` act only lightly so the field remains pre-scenic and any underway-ness stays only as faint pressure.
5. Let `phrase_rebinding_pressure` stay minimal so relations remain fixed, with only barely gathered role-field or presence pressure.
6. Let `continuity_loosening` keep the field sparse but not disconnected.
7. Let `contradiction_tolerance` stay low so incompatible hints do not harden into stable coexistence.
8. Let `micro_texture_distortion` add only slight wrongness to edges, surfaces, and small details.
9. Let `banal_intrusion_allowance` act only if mundane traces remain rare, small, pressure-bearing, and do not thicken into scene detail.

Rejection conditions
Reject any result where:
- the field becomes fully scenic too early
- weak pressure is over-expanded into stable world structure
- translation becomes explicit instead of faint
- the phase breaks its low-amplitude ceilings
- memory distortion becomes too strong for threshold phase
- instability outruns weak field formation
- encounter pressure becomes full staging
- role-field pressure becomes explicit role assignment
- affordance hints become scripted action
- contradiction thickens into stable incompatibility
- banal intrusion thickens into scene detail
